JEFF PIXEL▮

Pixel capitals on the square-cell grid of the Jeff logo: 7 cells tall, 2-cell vertical strokes, 1-cell horizontal strokes, square corners, letters 2 cells apart. One cell is 100 of the 1000 font units per em, so font-size = 10 × cell px gives whole-pixel cells: 20 px (2 px cells), 30 px (3), 40 px (4), 60 px (6). Lowercase letters draw the capitals.
